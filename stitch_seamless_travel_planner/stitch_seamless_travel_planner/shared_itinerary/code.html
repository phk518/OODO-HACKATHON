The Journey

Day 01 — Arrival

Touchdown in Zurich & Train to Lucerne

Arrive at ZRH and take the scenic rail route directly to Lucerne. Check into the lakeside boutique hotel and enjoy a sunset dinner overlooking the Chapel Bridge.

Day 02 — Peaks

Mount Pilatus Dragon Ride

Ascend via the world's steepest cogwheel railway. Spend the afternoon hiking the Tomlishorn trail for 360-degree views of the Central Alps.
